# Shard XP model (training v2)

Generated by `tools/ShardXPModel.py` from the game's own curves (`design/native_curves.csv`)
and the logged fights. Change an assumption there and re-run.

These are planning estimates at 100% shard XP, not measured playthrough guarantees.
The assumed player-level trajectory is not derived from this mod's five-skill average;
specialist builds can remain at a lower XP multiplier for much longer. See
[SPECIALIST_BALANCE.md](SPECIALIST_BALANCE.md) before using these fight counts as targets.

## 1. Native skill progression (from the game files)

- XP per skill level: 500 (level 2) rising ~6-8% per level to 21357 (level 60); **305,006 XP** to reach 60. Level 50 is only half of that (150,233).
- Every skill XP award is multiplied by the **player-level multiplier**: x0.48 up to level 15, x0.98 at 40, x1.80 from 50; and by the enemy-level damage curve (x1.00 -> x1.41). A late fight is worth ~3.5x an early one.
- Damage XP = damage curve x enemy rarity multiplier x % of the enemy's Health dealt x player-level
  multiplier (x0.5 for non-Power guns).
- Enemy rarity multipliers (same at every level): trash x0.25, weak x0.6, normal x1, rare x1.5, officer x1.5, elite x2, boss x10.
  Full Health of a normal enemy = 100 native XP at x1; a boss = 1000.
- The logged Solo XP (half-rate shotgun) = 1219 native XP at x1 full rate, i.e. about **12 normal-enemy Health bars** of damage per fight.

## 2. Fights in a playthrough (derived, assuming native is balanced)

- Logged fights: 87 s of combat; Solo (tech shotgun, half rate) **293 XP** per fight at x0.48 = **610** at x1.
- If a skill used all game is meant to hit 60 around player level 50 (end of main + sides + Phantom Liberty: ~60 h + 20-30 h), a playthrough has **~600 fights**
  (~7 per hour over ~85 h).

## 3. Shard budget

- Shard channel XP = **native formula x 0.1** (same multipliers), so a focused fight gives ~29 shard XP early, ~41 mid-game, ~102 late.
- 3 shards train at once: 600 fights x 3 slots x 70% efficiency = ~1260 focused shard-fights.
- Mastering a shard from the start of the game takes **206 focused fights**, from mid-game **126**; a playthrough masters **~8 of 16 shards**
  under these assumptions. Different builds, settings and farming change that estimate.

## 5. Channel rates (at multiplier 1; all are then x player-level multiplier)

A focused fight is worth **61 shard XP at x1** (87 s), so:

| Channel type | Rate | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Damage share (weapons, hacks, explosions) | native damage XP x 0.05: 5 per enemy Health bar x rarity (trash 1.25 ... boss 50) | the x0.1 share applied to the typical half-rate gun the fight value was measured with; one rate for every weapon |
| Time in a state (wounded, crouched near enemies, Overdrive) | 0.70 XP/s | 61 XP over a 87 s fight |
| Dash / dodge / slide / vault in combat | 4.9 XP each | you made 12.5 per fight |
| Queued quickhack | 3.8 XP per RAM | 2 queued per fight, ~8 RAM each |
| Health lost and survived | 20 XP per max-Health bar | ~1.5 bars per fight = half a fight's value |
| Events (Obliterate, finisher, item use, kill in Adrenaline Rush) | 12 XP each | 1-3 per fight |

## 6. Limiters

- Time and event channels: at most 28 XP (x multipliers) per 10 s window, 4x the average pace.
  Damage channels have no window cap; the per-target cap below bounds them.
- Per target/family: damage XP stops at 100% health share while its entry remains in the
  bounded session cache (160 entries; reset on reload). This limits repeated damage but
  is not a persistent anti-farming guarantee. A boss pays up to 50 shard XP at x1
  (x10, about 0.8 focused fights), an elite 10, trash 1.25.
- Time channels require combat except Ninjutsu Shadow, which requires nearby watchers
  before combat and stops paying after 5 seconds at one spot (move over 3 m to reset).
- The per-family 0-500% setting multiplies XP after the base limiter; fractional XP
  carries across actions and saves. At 0%, the carry and window budget are unchanged.
